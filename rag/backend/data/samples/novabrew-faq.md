# NovaBrew Aero Carafe — Product FAQ

NovaBrew Aero Carafe is a 1.2 liter thermal carafe coffee maker for home kitchens. It brews at 96°C, holds heat for four hours, and uses a stainless mesh basket instead of paper filters.

## Brew sizes

Single cup is 300 ml and takes three minutes. Half carafe is 600 ml. Full carafe is 1.2 liters and takes eight minutes. Do not start a brew cycle unless the tank has at least 250 ml of water.

## Descale schedule

Descale every 40 brew cycles or every 30 days, whichever comes first. Use 200 ml white vinegar mixed with 200 ml water. Run a brew with the basket empty, then run two plain-water rinses. Hard-water homes should descale every 20 cycles.

## Error codes

E1 means the lid sensor is open. Close the carafe lid until it clicks. E4 means the heater overheated because the tank was dry. Unplug the unit for ten minutes, refill the tank, then press the reset pinhole on the base for three seconds. E7 is a scale warning, not a failure.

## Warranty

The heating element is covered for 24 months. Glass is not covered against drops. Proof of purchase is required. Replacement mesh baskets are sold as part NB-44.

## Cleaning the mesh basket

Rinse the basket after every brew. Once a week, soak it in warm water with a teaspoon of baking soda for fifteen minutes, then rinse. Never put the basket in a dishwasher; the mesh warps above 80°C in dry heat.
