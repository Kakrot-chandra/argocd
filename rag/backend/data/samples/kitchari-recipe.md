# Kitchari for monsoon evenings

This pot feeds two adults. Kitchari is a one-pot stew of rice and mung dal used in Ayurvedic kitchens when digestion feels slow. This version is North Indian, not a medical protocol.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup split yellow mung dal, rinsed until the water runs almost clear
- 1/2 cup white basmati rice, rinsed
- 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
- 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 1 tablespoon ghee
- 4 cups water
- 1 teaspoon salt, added at the end
- Optional: handful of chopped spinach in the last three minutes

## Method

Warm the ghee in a heavy pot. Add cumin and mustard seeds and wait until the mustard pops. Stir in ginger for twenty seconds, then turmeric. Add dal, rice, and water. Simmer uncovered on low for 28–32 minutes, stirring so the bottom does not catch. The grains should melt together, not stay separate like pilaf. Finish with salt.

## What not to do

Do not use brown mung (whole green gram) for this recipe; it will not break down in 30 minutes. Do not add lemon until serving, or the dal stays grainy. Leftovers keep one day in the fridge; reheat with a splash of water.

## Serving

Eat with a spoon of ghee and black pepper. Pickles are fine; heavy fried sides defeat the point of a light monsoon meal.
