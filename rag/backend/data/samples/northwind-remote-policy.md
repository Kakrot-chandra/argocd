# Northwind Labs — Remote Work Policy (2026)

This policy applies to full-time employees of Northwind Labs in India, Germany, and the United States. Contractors follow their statement of work instead.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time, Monday through Thursday. Friday core hours are 11:00 to 14:00. Employees must be reachable on chat during core hours unless they logged PTO.

## Office cadence

Employees within 50 km of a Northwind office attend in person on Tuesdays. Exceptions require a manager note in Workday. Employees farther than 50 km are fully remote and visit twice per quarter for planning weeks.

## Equipment

Northwind ships one laptop, one 27-inch display, and a docking station. Home internet stipends are INR 1,500 / EUR 25 / USD 30 per month, paid with payroll. Ergonomic chairs are reimbursed up to USD 250 once every three years with a receipt.

## Security

Company data may not be stored on personal Google Drive or Dropbox. VPN is required on public networks. Screenshots of customer datasets must stay in the Northwind vault. Lost devices must be reported within two hours to security@northwind.example.

## Time off while remote

Remote employees use the same PTO bank as office employees: 22 days plus local holidays. Working from another country for more than 14 calendar days in a quarter needs People Ops approval for tax reasons.
